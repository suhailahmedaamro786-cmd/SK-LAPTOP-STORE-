# Premium storefront UX skill

- Follow docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md and docs/PHASE-1-FOUNDATION.md.
- Use the exact approved logo asset; if absent, request it rather than fabricating one.
- Design mobile-first for Pakistan's mixed device/network conditions.
- Make product condition, price, stock and delivery uncertainty explicit.
- Use semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible forms and WCAG-minded contrast.
- Provide loading, empty, error and unavailable states.
- Never fabricate product details, trust badges, reviews or stock.
- Keep admin-only information out of all storefront payloads and UI.
