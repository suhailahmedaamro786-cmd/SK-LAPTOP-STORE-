# SK LAPTOP STORE — Design system direction

## Brand
Use the exact approved SK LAPTOP STORE logo asset consistently in the storefront header, footer, favicon, social preview and order communications. Do not redraw, recolor, distort or invent a replacement logo. The repository currently has no verified logo asset; add the owner's approved logo before final brand implementation.

Brand attribution copy (approved wording): **Owned by Mr. Suhail Ahmed Aamro, Founder of SK DEV TEAM.** Optional brand-story section may celebrate his early entrepreneurial work for his family and Pakistan, but must remain respectful, factual and free of exaggerated claims.

## Visual direction
Premium technology retail: confident, clean, modern, high-contrast and product-led. Use a restrained neutral foundation with one approved brand accent. Prioritize real laptop photography, precise typography, generous spacing, crisp cards, strong product comparison and clear purchase actions. Avoid clutter, excessive gradients, fake trust badges and decorative effects that compete with products.

## UX requirements
- Consistent desktop and mobile navigation; accessible mobile menu.
- Product cards show price, condition, availability and concise key specs.
- Used devices display an explicit condition grade and individual notes.
- Checkout keeps fees and payment instructions transparent.
- Admin-only data never appears in public components.
- WCAG-minded contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus and semantic landmarks.

## Design tokens
Define color, typography, spacing, radius, shadow, z-index and motion tokens centrally before building pages. Do not hard-code unrelated values across components. Use a shared component library for buttons, inputs, badges, dialogs, tables, cards and empty states.
