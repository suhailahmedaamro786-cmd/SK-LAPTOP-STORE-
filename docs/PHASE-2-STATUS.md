# Phase 2 — Storefront UI foundation

## Implemented in this phase branch
- Refined responsive homepage with catalogue search form and category navigation.
- Added catalogue route with new/used filters and honest empty state.
- Added contact page that clearly discloses that official contact channels are not configured.
- Added draft policy page covering product accuracy, payment, shipping, checking/returns, privacy and warranty disclosures.
- Reused the existing SK LAPTOP STORE SVG asset and retained founder attribution.
- Added responsive styles and reduced-motion support without adding heavy animation dependencies.

## Not implemented yet
- Live inventory, product detail records, cart, checkout, order tracking or customer accounts.
- WhatsApp integration, payment gateway/bank reconciliation or courier API.
- Final approved returns, checking, privacy and warranty terms.
- Production build verification and deployment.

## Safety
No sample stock, product prices, customer reviews or delivery promises are fabricated. The catalogue shows an explicit empty state until verified inventory is connected. Contact channels remain pending configuration.
